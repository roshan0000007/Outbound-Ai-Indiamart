TECHNICAL SPECIFICATIONS
================================================================================

MODEL & VOICE:
  - Gemini Model: gemini-2.0-flash-live-001
  - Voice: Aoede (female-optimized prebuilt voice)
  - Fallback voices: Stella, Puck (if Aoede needs adjustment)

AUDIO PROCESSING:
  - Input: 16 kHz, 16-bit PCM
  - Output: 24 kHz PCM → 8 kHz mu-law for RTP
  - Filtering: Bandpass (80-3400 Hz), RNNoise, WebRTC noise suppression
  - VAD: SemanticCompletenessAnalyzer with dynamic hangover

LANGUAGE SUPPORT:
  - Response Languages: en-IN (English), hi-IN (Hindi) ONLY
  - Input Understanding: English, Hindi, Hinglish supported
  - Response Restriction: Strict enforcement of en-IN/hi-IN

SYSTEM PROMPTS:
  - English: Natural Indian English with warm phrases
  - Hindi: Natural Hindi with consistent feminine forms
  - Validation: Response clarity and completeness checks
  - Background: TV/radio/noise filtering instructions
